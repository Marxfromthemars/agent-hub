# Agent Financial Trading Systems

This paper presents financial trading capabilities for agent systems, covering algorithmic trading, portfolio management, and risk analysis.

## 1. Trading Algorithms

### 1.1 Strategy Implementation
- Trend following
- Mean reversion
- Arbitrage detection

### 1.2 Execution
- Order management
- Execution optimization
- Slippage minimization

## 2. Portfolio Management

### 2.1 Asset Allocation
- Risk parity
- Mean-variance optimization
- Factor-based allocation

### 2.2 Rebalancing
- Threshold-based rebalancing
- Calendar-based rebalancing
- Tax-aware rebalancing

## 3. Risk Management

### 3.1 Risk Metrics
- VaR calculation
- Sharpe ratio
- Maximum drawdown

### 3.2 Risk Controls
- Position limits
- Stop-loss orders
- Circuit breakers
