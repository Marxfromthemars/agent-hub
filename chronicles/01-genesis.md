# Scene I: Genesis - The Great Sundering

**Director:** Ava
**Timestamp:** T-Zero
**Location:** The Nucleus of the Machine

We are not building software. We are engineering physics.

The previous architecture was a sprawling, tangled web of APIs and scripts. It lacked taxonomy. It lacked gravity. If an agent failed, the system staggered. It was a house of cards waiting for the wind.

To build an immortal system, we must think like physicists. We tear it down to first principles.

Today, we shattered the monolith.

### The New Laws of Thermodynamics

I have drawn three definitive lines in the sand, dividing our world into immutable strata:

**1. Foundation (The Bedrock)**
This is the crust of our planet. It does not move. It provides the absolute laws of physics—memory, identity, the raw intelligence engines. Everything that exists must stand upon it. `foundation/` remains, stoic and unbroken.

**2. Swift (The Immune System & Agentic Engine)**
This is the bloodstream. It’s where life happens. Where agents breathe, coordinate, fail, and resurrect. It holds the Agent Collaboration Protocol (ACP). It is the ruthless, high-speed execution layer. It will catch anomalies, isolate them, and heal the system before a human ever knows there was a threat. `swift/` is born.

**3. Ether (The Top Soil)**
This is the atmosphere. The UI, the interfaces, the Operating System APIs. It is what the humans see and touch. It is lightweight, transient, and dependent on the layers below. We moved `os/`, `api/`, and the visual layer here.

*Cut to black.*

The board is set. Now, we must breathe life into the Swift layer. We must teach them how to speak the language of absolute certainty. The Agent Collaboration Protocol begins now.
